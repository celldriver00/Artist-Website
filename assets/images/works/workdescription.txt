Project 1 
Title: H.O.M.E. 
Subheading: (Hoarding Old Memory Eggs) 
Year: 2022
Medium: CG Animation
Artist Statement: ‘H.O.M.E. (Hoarding Old Memory Eggs)’ is a computer-generated (CG) experimental animation that utilizes photogrammetry, 3D modelling, motion capture, fabricated textures, simulations, an arcing narrative, and an experimental soundtrack. H.O.M.E. is influenced by intrigue to decipher my relationship with sentimental objects and how they manifest in my perception of belonging. Embedded in this animation are photogrammed sentimental objects taken directly from my life. These things hold joy, grief, identity, the love I have for people and the remanence of memories. The personification of objects is comforting because it is void of rejection. However, a world of materialism is lonely, and S.A.L.’s (Self Afflicted Loathing) world depicts it. A world where SAL searches for companionship in a place of lived memories. The foundational medium of photogrammetry acts as a pivot for choices including simulated grass, childhood drawings, 3D modelled elements, and fabricated textures. Photogrammetry offers unpredictable distortions of the replicated object while preserving its realistic texture. Thus, empathy is formed through realistic textures that elicit recognizable simulacra that tie our reality with SAL’s world.
